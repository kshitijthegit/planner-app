Privacy policy

Planner is a personal productivity app used by a single person, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Google account email — only to check that the person signing in is the owner.
  • Google Calendar (read-only) — events are copied into the app's private database so they can be shown next to tasks and used for reminders. The app never creates, edits or deletes anything in Google Calendar.

How data is stored and shared

Data is stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database belonging to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Push notifications are delivered through the browser's push service (for example, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for Chrome).

Removing data

Disconnecting the app at Google Account → Third-party access stops all access. Synced events can be removed by deleting the calendar source in the app's settings.
